# Lifestyle image prompts

Eight images for the Astro Energy Group site. Save each one in `src/assets/lifestyle/` with the **exact filename** shown. A `.png` or `.jpg` with the same basename also works; run `npm run images:webp` to make a proper WebP conversion. The site picks up each image on the next build and uses a fallback photo until then. Placements, focal points and alt text are in `src/data/images.ts`.

**These images are illustrative.** Don't caption or describe the people as Astro staff or clients, or the places as Astro offices or projects.

## Shared style (already included in every prompt below)

Editorial documentary photography, natural daylight, believable people of varied ages and backgrounds in real work clothing, realistic contemporary buildings, rich but restrained color, with subtle deep navy and teal accents in clothing or surroundings. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 35–50 mm lens and moderate depth of field. Unstaged, candid expressions. No text, signage, logos, brand names, watermarks or legible screens. Not glossy or over-retouched, no "stock smile" poses, no surreal lighting.

## 1. `astro-home-hero.webp`

- **Placement:** Homepage hero, right column; runs to the viewport edge on desktop
- **Recommended size:** 2400 × 1350 px
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Prompt:**
  > Editorial documentary photograph of two facilities engineers on a commercial building rooftop on a clear morning, standing beside large air-handling units and insulated ductwork. One, a woman in her forties wearing a navy work jacket and safety glasses, holds a tablet angled toward her colleague. He is a man in his thirties in a hard hat and teal high-visibility vest, pointing toward the equipment as they discuss it. Both are on the right two-thirds of the frame. The left third is open sky and softly out-of-focus rooftop equipment. Natural daylight, believable people, realistic building, rich but restrained color with navy and teal accents, 35 mm lens, moderate depth of field, candid expressions, no text, no logos, no legible screen content, no watermarks.
- **Crop guidance:** Keep both people and the tablet inside the right 60% of the frame, faces at roughly 40% from the top. On desktop the site crops toward a near-square from the right (focal point 72% across, 45% down). On mobile it shows a 16:10 crop. Leave quiet space on the left.

Mechanical and floor-plan drawings are spread on the table, with a laptop and coffee cups. A man in his fifties in a navy sweater points at a drawing. ## 3. `astro-building-life.webp`

- **Placement:** Full-width band on the About page; also on the homepage once supplied
- **Recommended size:** 2400 × 1350 px (it is cropped to about 21:9 on wide screens)
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9
- **Prompt:**
  > Wide editorial photograph of a bright, comfortable multi-story building atrium in the afternoon. Daylight pours through a glass roof and tall windows. People are going about their day: two colleagues talking on a staircase, someone working on a laptop at a long wooden table, a person walking through with a coffee, and indoor plants. The building feels calm, well-ventilated and comfortable, with even temperature and no glare. The people are small to medium in the frame and the architecture is the main subject. Natural daylight, believable people, realistic contemporary architecture, rich but restrained color with navy and teal accents in furniture or clothing, 24–35 mm lens, no text, no logos, no signage, no watermarks.
- **Crop guidance:** Keep the main human activity in a horizontal band between 35% and 75% of the height, because wide screens crop the top and bottom. On mobile it is cropped to 4:3 from the center, so keep at least one person near the middle.

After adding images:
1. If you saved PNG or JPG files, run `npm run images:webp`.
2. Compare each image with its alt text in `src/data/images.ts` and edit the alt text if the image differs.
3. Run `npm run build`, then commit and push.
